# GBSB4U Digital Parliament – Proposal Lifecycle (v1.0)

## 1. Proposal Initiation
- Any verified Digital Citizen may raise an issue
- Issues are submitted to the Citizen Chamber
- Moderators ensure clarity and relevance

## 2. Deliberation Phase
- Open deliberation in the Citizen Chamber
- Diverse viewpoints are encouraged
- Abuse, propaganda, or disruption is filtered

## 3. Policy Drafting
- Viable issues move to the Policy Chamber
- Domain contributors draft structured proposals
- Alternatives and impacts are documented

## 4. AI-Assisted Analysis
AI may assist with:
- Data aggregation
- Impact simulation
- Bias and risk identification

AI shall not:
- Decide outcomes
- Replace human reasoning

## 5. Review & Ethics Check
- Review & Ethics Chamber checks:
  - Constitutional compliance
  - Justice and dignity impact
  - Ethical risks

## 6. Decision Stage
- Decisions seek reasoned consensus
- If consensus fails, documented judgment is required
- Founder authority intervenes only in constitutional conflict

## 7. Outcome
A proposal may be:
- Accepted
- Rejected (with reasons)
- Sent back for revision

## 8. Transparency & Record
- Entire lifecycle is recorded
- Public reasoning accompanies decisions
- No anonymous approvals are allowed
