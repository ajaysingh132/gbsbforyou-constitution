# AI Assistance Rules – GBSB4U Digital Parliament (v1.0)

## 1. Role of AI
Artificial Intelligence in the GBSB4U Digital Parliament
exists solely as an assistive tool.

AI has no authority, sovereignty, or decision-making power.

## 2. Permitted Uses of AI
AI may be used for:
- Summarizing debates and documents
- Data analysis and pattern detection
- Impact and scenario simulation
- Identifying delays, inconsistencies, or risks

## 3. Prohibited Uses of AI
AI shall never:
- Decide policy outcomes
- Vote or rank proposals
- Override human judgment
- Act without clear human accountability

## 4. Human Accountability
Every AI-assisted output must have:
- A clearly identified human owner
- Human verification and approval
- Recorded reasoning for acceptance or rejection

## 5. Transparency & Auditability
- AI inputs and outputs must be traceable
- No black-box decisions are permitted
- AI systems are subject to regular review

## 6. Bias, Safety, and Ethics
- Known biases must be documented
- Outputs affecting justice or dignity require heightened scrutiny
- Harmful or misleading outputs must be discarded

## 7. Override & Shutdown
Human authority may override or suspend AI assistance
at any time in the interest of justice, ethics, or safety.
