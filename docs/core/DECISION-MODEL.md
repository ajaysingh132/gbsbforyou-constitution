# GBSB4U Digital Parliament – Decision Model (v1.0)

## 1. Guiding Principle
Decisions must be reasoned, transparent, and accountable.
Speed or convenience cannot override justice and wisdom.

## 2. Preference for Consensus
- The primary goal is reasoned consensus
- Diverse viewpoints are reconciled through dialogue
- Consensus must be documented with reasons

## 3. Qualified Decision (When Consensus Fails)
When consensus is not achievable:
- A qualified decision may be taken
- Minority viewpoints must be recorded
- Justification is mandatory

## 4. Constitutional Safeguard & Veto
- Founder / Constitutional Authority may exercise veto only when:
  - A proposal violates the GBSB4U Constitution
  - Justice, dignity, or civilizational principles are threatened
- All vetoes must be reasoned and documented

## 5. Accountability Chain
Every decision must clearly identify:
- Contributors
- Reviewers
- Final decision authority

Anonymous decisions are prohibited.

## 6. Transparency & Public Record
- Decisions include written reasoning
- Records are preserved for audit and learning
- Amendments follow versioned updates

## 7. Role of AI
AI may:
- Summarize arguments
- Highlight risks and inconsistencies

AI may not:
- Decide outcomes
- Override human accountability
