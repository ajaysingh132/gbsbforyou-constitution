# GBSB4U Digital Parliament – Structure (v1.0)

## 1. Nature of the Digital Parliament
The GBSB4U Digital Parliament is a non-electoral, non-party,
role-based deliberative system.

It is designed to replace adversarial politics
with reasoned public decision-making.

## 2. Core Chambers

### 2.1 Citizen Chamber
- Open to verified Digital Citizens
- Space for issue raising and public deliberation
- No voting authority, only deliberative input

### 2.2 Policy Chamber
- Composed of subject-domain contributors
- Responsible for drafting and refining proposals
- Decisions based on reasoned consensus

### 2.3 Review & Ethics Chamber
- Reviews proposals for constitutional compliance
- Ensures justice, dignity, and ethical alignment
- Can send proposals back for revision

## 3. Roles Within the Parliament

### 3.1 Member
- Participates in discussions and drafting
- Bound by code of conduct

### 3.2 Moderator
- Ensures discipline and constructive dialogue
- Cannot influence outcomes

### 3.3 Analyst
- Provides data, impact analysis, and simulations
- May use AI-assisted tools (advisory only)

### 3.4 Observer
- Non-participatory transparency role
- No decision-making power

## 4. Oversight & Authority
- The Parliament operates within the GBSB4U Constitution
- Founder / Constitutional Authority intervenes only in:
  - Constitutional conflict
  - Civilizational or ethical violations

## 5. Transparency Principle
- All deliberations are recorded
- Decisions must be reasoned and documented
- No anonymous authority is permitted
