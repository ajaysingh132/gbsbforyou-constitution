GOVERNANCE TEAMS

Local & Main Governance

Local Governance Team

Works independently on the needs of its Circle while remaining connected to the wider governance network.

  • Local priorities
  • Community programs
  • Member reporting
  • Local accountability

Main Governance Team

Coordinates common and national-level community work without unnecessarily controlling legitimate local decisions.

  • National coordination
  • Shared programs
  • Resources
  • Network reporting

Team Leader

The Team Leader is selected through the approved process and is accountable to the Governance Team and the represented community.
